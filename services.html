  • Dedicated driver for the length of your stay
  • Mercedes V-Class or S-Class as standard, others on request
  • Unlimited radius across Mykonos · 24-hour availability

  • From €1,400 / day · multi-day rates on request

Service 02

Per-Route Transfers

Quick, punctual, fixed-fare transfers between any two points on the island. Tracked flights, ferries, and yacht ETAs. A chauffeur waiting before you arrive.

  • JMK Airport ↔ any villa, hotel, or port
  • Old Port ↔ New Port (Tourlos) ↔ Rafina / Piraeus ferries
  • Beach club & restaurant round-trips with wait time
  • Meet & greet, name board, luggage handled
  • From €120 per route, all-in

On-Disposal Driver

A chauffeur and luxury vehicle reserved for a defined window — three hours, twelve hours, or the whole day. They wait, they re-park, they re-route on a whim.

  • Hourly (3-hour minimum) · half-day (6h) · full-day (12h)

  • Wait time included — restaurant, beach, shopping
  • From €110 / hour · daily packages from €950
