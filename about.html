About us · Since 2018

A small island.
An obsessive standard.

MPM is a family-run house of chauffeurs, captains and concierges. We have lived on Mykonos all our lives, and we treat every guest as the only guest of the season.

Our story

Eight summers,
one quiet promise.

Alexandros Mavros founded MPM after fifteen years driving the island's diplomatic and yacht clientele. The premise was small: arrive on time, every time. Speak softly. Know the route before the guest does. Replace the car before it ever looks tired.

Today, MPM is the chauffeur partner to several of Mykonos' top hotels and most-loved villas. We have transported newlyweds, heads of state, family reunions, and weekend houseguests with the same standard — punctual, discreet, and pleasant.

The fleet is brand-new and replaced every twenty-four months. The drivers are vetted locals. The phone is always answered. And the rest is a matter of doing it again, tomorrow.

How we work

Six house rules,
kept religiously.

These aren't policies for a brochure. They are the things we audit at the end of each season — and the reason guests return for theirs.

On time, always early

Chauffeurs arrive fifteen minutes before pickup. We track flights, ferries, and yacht ETAs in real time.

Confidentiality, by contract

Every chauffeur signs an NDA. No photographs, no social media, no chatter at the bar.

Brand-new fleet

Vehicles are retired at 24 months of service or 60,000 kilometres — whichever comes first.

One concierge per guest

You speak to a single person for the length of your stay. The phone is answered, day or night.

Local knowledge, not Google

Our chauffeurs are island-born. They know the chef at Nammos, the marina manager at Tourlos, and the quiet way to Elia.

One call resolves it

Lost luggage at JMK, a late departure from Scorpios, a last-minute helicopter. One number, one answer.
